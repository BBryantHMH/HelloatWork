Crisis lines

  • 988 Lifeline, call or text
  • Crisis Text Line, text HOME to 741741
  • Veterans: 988, then press 1
  • En español: 988, then press 2
  • Deaf or hard of hearing: 711, then 988
  • Not in crisis but need to talk? Ohio CareLine, 1-800-720-9616
