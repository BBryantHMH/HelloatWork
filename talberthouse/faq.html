Privacy & confidentiality

Your privacy, answered plainly.

The most common questions about how the Mental Health Check-Up protects your privacy — what stays anonymous, what is kept, and what Talbert House and your health plan can and cannot see.

Two steps, two levels of privacy.

The online Self-Check questionnaire is completely anonymous and saves nothing. The Check-Up — your 30-minute visit with a licensed psychologist — does create a private, protected health record, the same as any visit to a psychologist. The questions below explain both.

Is the Self-Check anonymous?

Yes, the online questionnaire is fully anonymous. We don’t store your name, e-mail, or any information that ties your results to your identity.

Take the Self-Check
What happens to the information I share during the Check-Up?

We keep a short record of your Check-Up, the same way any psychologist’s office would. It includes:

  • Your survey results
  • Your psychologist’s notes
  • Your written summary

The law protects this record. It’s protected health information under HIPAA and Ohio law, the same protection you’d get at any visit with a licensed psychologist.

There are only three exceptions when your private information would be shared. These are the times when someone’s safety is at stake or the law requires us to act:

  • A safety risk
  • Suspected abuse
  • A court order
Read our Notice of Privacy Practices
What if my supervisor asks if I came to Hello Mental Health?

Your psychologist can’t tell anyone at Talbert House whether they know you. They can’t confirm it or deny it. That includes your supervisor.

The only way this changes is if you give your direct written consent. It’s your private information and we protect your privacy.

What does Talbert House see?

Talbert House can’t see the personal information Hello Mental Health keeps as part of your care with us.

What we do share are general trends, like:

  • Aggregated data such as how many staff are using the services
  • Data shared are for whole groups of people and services. No individual information is shared.

Talbert House uses this information to see how current programs are working and to plan future wellness efforts. These numbers never include your name or anything that could identify you.

What does my health plan see?

Only what it needs to process a claim:

  • The dates of your visits
  • Service and diagnostic codes (short codes that say what kind of visit it was and why)
  • Routine billing information
Still have a question about your privacy?

Reach out to the Hello Mental Health team — we’re glad to walk through any of this with you before you book. And if you ever need support right now, the crisis lines at the top and bottom of this page are always available.
