Need to talk to someone right now?

Call or text 988. Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free, 24/7. You don't have to be suicidal to reach them.
Text HOME to 741741. Crisis Text Line, 24/7.
Call 911 for any immediate emergency.

This tool is not a crisis service and is not monitored, and finishing it never triggers an alert. Nothing you type is ever stored or linked to you. If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, please use one of the options above.
