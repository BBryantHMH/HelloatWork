Program pillars

How we support Talbert House staff

The Hello Mental Health workplace wellness program is built around four pillars that address the specific stressors of human services work.

📱

Mental Health Check-Ups

Anonymous questionnaires + 30-minute sessions with a licensed clinician. Launched August 2026 with the new health plan.

👥

Quarterly Forums

Live skill-building sessions covering burnout, secondary trauma, team dynamics, and self-care. Open to all staff.

📚

Resource Library

Vetted crisis lines, provider directories, apps, and reading lists — curated for the kinds of challenges you actually face.

🆕

Reflective Practice

Tools and prompts to build reflective supervision habits within teams, not just in designated sessions.
