Throughout the day

Values Anchor

Prompt< 2 min

Real Support · A Talbert House and Hello Mental Health partnership · Wellness On-Demand

On hard days, the reason you do this work can get buried. Choosing one word that captures it gives you something steady to come back to.

Step by step

  1. Think about why you chose this work, or why you stay.
  2. Pick one word that captures it (for example: compassion, dignity, hope, justice, second chances).
  3. Write it somewhere you'll see it: a sticky note, your badge, your phone's lock screen.
  4. When the day gets hard, look at your word and take one breath.

Tips

  • Your word can change over time. Choose what fits this season.
  • Some teams share their words with each other; it can be a quick team exercise.
