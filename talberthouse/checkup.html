New with the August 2026 health plan

Your yearly Mental Health Check-Up.

You get an annual physical for your body. Starting this plan year, you can get one for your mental health too — private, simple, and on your schedule.

How it works

Two steps. Both simple. Both yours to choose.

1

Wellness Questionnaire

A short, non-clinical self-check across areas of life — stress, sleep, mood, relationships, work. Takes a few minutes and flags anything worth a closer look.

Completely anonymous. Nothing is shared with your health plan or with Talbert House unless you choose to book a check-up.

FreeAnonymous~5 minutes
Coming with the new plan year. The questionnaire opens here in August 2026.
2

Mental Health Check-Up

A scheduled 30-minute visit with a licensed psychologist at Hello Mental Health: validated screening measures, a personal review of your results, tailored recommendations, and a written summary to keep.

Designed as a preventive-care benefit under the new Talbert House health plan — think of it as an annual physical for your mental health. Coverage details will be confirmed when the plan launches. Appointments within one week. In person (Mt. Auburn) or telehealth.

Preventive care30 minutesWithin 1 weekTalbert House Health Plan
Online booking opens August 2026. Until then, call (513) 717-5566 with questions.
What happens after a check-up?

One of two things: you're doing well and leave with your summary — or, if support would help, you get warm referrals matched to your needs. That may include Hello Mental Health, or another trusted provider in the community. The choice is always yours.
