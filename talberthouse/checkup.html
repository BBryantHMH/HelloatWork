Your Mental Health Check‑Up

You spend your days showing up for other people. This is a little time that’s just for you: a private, 30-minute conversation with a licensed psychologist about how you’re really doing. Open to all Talbert House staff, and to spouses and dependents 18 and older on the Talbert House health plan.

Schedule your Check-Up → Take the Self-Check first
What to expect

A check-in, not a commitment.

Think of it as a consultation for the curious. You’ll sit down with one of our psychologists and talk about how things have been lately: stress, sleep, mood, work, the weight you might be carrying home. There are no wrong answers, and nothing you need to prepare to say. Check-Ups are provided by Hello Mental Health’s licensed psychologists; you can meet the team here.

It isn’t therapy, and it doesn’t sign you up for anything. You’ll leave with a clearer picture of how you’re doing, a few ideas that might help, and an honest sense of whether more support could be useful down the road.

You don’t need to be struggling to book one, and you don’t need to take the Self-Check first, though it can be a nice way to warm up.

  • ⏱30 minutes with a licensed psychologist
  • 🔒Private and confidential. We never share your records with Talbert House
  • ✓Covered at 100% by the Talbert House health plan
  • 📅Request online, anytime, in just a few minutes
How it works

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Request your appointment

    Request a day and time that works for you through our online scheduler.

  2. 2

    Complete your paperwork

    Once your appointment is confirmed, we’ll email you the consent form, questionnaires, and intake documents. Please finish all of them before your appointment. Want to read the consent form first? Just ask.

    Required
  3. 3

    Meet your psychologist

    Join your 30-minute Check-Up and simply talk about how you’ve been doing.

  4. 4

    Leave with next steps

    Walk away with a few practical ideas, and options for more support if you’d like them.

Please complete everything before your appointment.

Your questionnaires and intake documents help your psychologist understand where you are before you even say hello, so your 30 minutes can be spent on you rather than on forms.

If any questionnaires or intake documents are still incomplete at your appointment time, we won’t be able to hold your Check-Up, and we’ll need to reschedule. We know that’s disappointing, so we recommend finishing them as soon as you book.

Can’t find the email or having trouble with a form? Send us a note or call (513) 717-5566 and we’ll help.

Cost

Covered by the Talbert House plan.

We want cost to be the last thing on your mind, so here’s exactly how it works.

100%

Covered by the Talbert House plan

If you’re on the Talbert House health plan, as an employee, spouse, or dependent 18 or older, your Check-Up is covered in full.

$150

If coverage is denied

In the rare case your plan denies coverage for any reason, the fee for the Check-Up is $150. We’ll always be upfront with you if that happens.

Superbill

If you’re paying on your own

For self-pay clients, we’ll provide a superbill you can submit to your own insurance for possible reimbursement.

Like any visit billed to insurance, your health plan receives the information it needs to process the claim, such as visit dates and service codes. If you’d rather your Check-Up not go through the plan, you can pay the $150 yourself.

After your Check-Up

If you want ongoing therapy.

Some people leave the Check-Up with what they need. Others decide they’d like regular support. Either is a good outcome.

Hello Mental Health is the preferred provider for the Talbert House health plan. For anyone 18 or older on the plan, therapy with one of our psychologists has a $15 copay. To get started, you can self-schedule or send us a note, or call (513) 717-5566.

You’re always free to choose another provider. The Mental Health Care page has therapist finders and local options, and your plan can tell you what they’ll cost.

48h
Cancellation policy

Life happens. Just give us 48 hours.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please let us know at least 48 hours before your appointment. That gives us time to offer your spot to another Talbert House colleague who’s waiting to be seen.

Appointments cancelled with less than 48 hours’ notice, or missed without notice, may be subject to a fee under our cancellation policy. To cancel or reschedule, use the link in your confirmation email, send us a note, or call (513) 717-5566.

Like any visit billed to insurance, your health plan receives the information it needs to process the claim, such as visit dates and service codes. If you’d rather your Check-Up not go through the plan, you can pay the $150 yourself.

Ready when you are.

Request your Check-Up online in a few minutes. Not sure yet? Try the private Self-Check, or reach out with any questions and a real person from our team will get back to you within one business day.
