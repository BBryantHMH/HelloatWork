About the program.

Real Support is a mental health and wellness program for Talbert House staff, delivered by the psychologists at Hello Mental Health. It brings together the Self-Check, Mental Health Check-Ups, mental health care resources, Wellness On-Demand tools, quarterly forums, and a recovery support group.

How this started

This partnership started with a conversation and a lot of heart. Talbert House came to us wanting to do something good for their people, the ones who show up every day for clients, families & this community and don’t always get that same care back. We understood what they were looking for, and we’re honored to be the ones delivering it. Hello Mental Health is a group of psychologists here in Cincinnati, and for the last ten years we’ve been trying to make therapy feel less like a sterile office and more like talking to someone who actually gets it. We’re big on real conversations, we laugh a lot, and we think asking for support is one of the bravest and most ordinary things a person can do. So we sat down together and built Real Support. We hope it feels like what it is: two teams who care about you, trying to make it a little easier to take care of yourself.
