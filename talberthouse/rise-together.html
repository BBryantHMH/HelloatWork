Rise Together

A recovery support group

You spend your days helping other people find their footing. Rise Together is a place to steady your own. Once a month, Talbert House staff in recovery come together online with a licensed psychologist to share, listen, and keep going, together.

Sign up for Rise Together ↓ See the dates
What it is

You don’t have to carry it alone.

Recovery takes daily effort, and working in human services can make it feel complicated in ways that are hard to explain to people outside the field. Rise Together is a small, supportive group made up of your Talbert House colleagues who understand both sides of that.

Each month, Dr. Rachel Thompson, a licensed psychologist with Hello Mental Health, guides the conversation. Some months you might want to talk; other months you might just want to listen. Both are welcome.

Wherever you are in your recovery, whether you’re just starting out or you’ve been at it for years, there’s a place for you here.

  • 📅Third Friday of every month at 11:00am Eastern
  • 💻Online, so you can join from wherever you are
  • 🤝Led by Dr. Rachel Thompson, a licensed psychologist
  • ✓No cost to Talbert House staff
  • 🔒Confidential. Talbert House never sees who signs up or attends

How it works

Three simple steps.

  1. 1

    Sign up below

    Fill out the short form on this page. It only takes a minute, and it goes straight to the Hello Mental Health team.

  2. 2

    Get the details

    We’ll follow up with everything you need to join the online session.

  3. 3

    Join the group

    Log on at 11:00am Eastern on the third Friday. Share as much or as little as you like.

Sign up

Save your spot.

Questions before you sign up? Send us a note or call (513) 717-5566, and a real person from our team will get back to you.

Private and confidential. Your sign-up goes directly to Hello Mental Health, and Talbert House never sees who signs up or attends. We ask that what’s shared in the group stays in the group.
Support, not therapy. Rise Together is a support group guided by a psychologist. It isn’t therapy of any kind, group or individual, and it isn’t a replacement for individual treatment or crisis care. If you’d like more support, we’re happy to share options, including a private Mental Health Check-Up.

Need support before the next session?

You don’t have to wait for the third Friday. Book a private Mental Health Check-Up with a licensed psychologist, or find recovery resources and support groups on the Mental Health Care page. If you’re in crisis, call or text 988 any time, or call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357.
