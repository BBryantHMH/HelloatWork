The landscape

The case for this program, in numbers.

Talbert House staff show up across addictions, community care, court & corrections, housing, mental health, and primary care. The weight of that work is real in every one of those roles. Here’s what the research shows.

93%
of behavioral health workers say they’ve experienced burnout, and 62% rate it moderate to severe. (National Council for Mental Wellbeing / Harris Poll, 2023)
27%
of corrections professionals screen positive for PTSD symptoms, roughly four times the general population.
48%
of adults living with a mental health condition didn’t get any treatment in the past year. (SAMHSA, National Survey on Drug Use and Health, 2024)
900+
Talbert House staff across 75 locations in Southwest Ohio.
