The landscape

The case for this library, in numbers.

Behavioral health work carries weight that doesn’t make it into case notes. Here’s what the research shows.

93%
of behavioral health workers say they have experienced burnout — 62% rate it moderate to severe. (National Council for Mental Wellbeing / Harris Poll, 2023)
23%
of U.S. adults live with a mental illness — more than 1 in 5. (NIMH, 2022)
30–60%
annual staff turnover at community behavioral health agencies — far above the ~10% considered healthy. (Clinical Child & Family Psychology Review, 2020)
900+

Talbert House at a glance

Five service lines. One community of care.

From addictions to housing, from court & corrections to mental health and primary care, Talbert House has helped Southwest Ohioans since 1965. This library is designed to support every one of those service lines.

Housing

Safe, stable housing including recovery and reentry housing programs.

Addictions

Outpatient, residential, and jail-based SUD treatment with MAT.

Mental health

Counseling, crisis services, and integrated behavioral healthcare.

Court & corrections

Reintegration services for justice-involved adults.

Community care

Case management, fatherhood, and community well-being programs.

Primary care

Integrated primary medical care for whole-person wellness.

Information reflects Talbert House’s public service framework. talberthouse.org
